# Managed Website Offer Specification

## Product

Two managed website subscriptions for Canadian service businesses that need a clear, trustworthy place to send customers.

- **Showcase — $79/month:** one-page mobile site with the business presentation, services and prices, service area and map, call/SMS/WhatsApp buttons, contact form, domain, hosting, SSL, content updates, and text/email support.
- **Booking — $129/month:** everything in Showcase, plus a booking or quote-request form with automatic confirmation and a Google reviews page.

Both plans include launch in about two weeks. The base site is in one language chosen by the client.

The initial commitment is 12 months. After that, the subscription continues month to month with no new commitment and 30 days’ notice. Early departure means either a $500 transfer fee, after which the site becomes the client’s property, or site deactivation. The domain always belongs to the client.

## One-time options

- The base site is written in one language of the client’s choice (English or French), included. Bilingual sites are discussed by custom quote.
- Extra page: $150 per page.
- Simple logo: $150.

## Delivery and care

- The client supplies accurate business facts, services, contact details, photos, and any existing logo.
- We validate the plan and details together before work begins.
- Orlando prepares the page, connects the domain, checks the contact paths, and launches after a short client review.
- Content updates and support by text or email are included while the subscription is active.
- Optional third-party services beyond the included domain, hosting, and SSL are paid separately when applicable.

## Boundaries

The standard service excludes ecommerce, online payments, custom applications, accounts, integrations, advertising, ongoing SEO or content campaigns, email administration, emergency support, unlimited redesigns, and complicated legacy-site rescue. Additional work is quoted before it begins.

## Audience and positioning

- Public service area: Canada.
- Strongest fits: businesses with no website, a Facebook/Google-only presence, or an outdated/basic site.
- Trust position: direct access to Orlando, local roots near Montreal, and clear willingness to refer agency-scale work elsewhere.

## Conversion and measurement

The primary action is a short Netlify managed-website request form, with WhatsApp and SMS as the fastest contact options. The form records the page locale, offer version, and campaign tags so private operations can interpret each submission against the terms the prospect saw. Umami Cloud is optional and enabled only when `PUBLIC_UMAMI_WEBSITE_ID` is configured.

Tracked signals:

- Visits and referral sources.
- Primary contact CTA clicks.
- WhatsApp and SMS CTA clicks.
- Managed-website form submission attempts.
- Localized thank-you-page completions.
