# Managed Website Messaging Guide

## Core promise

### English

**Your business deserves more than a Facebook page — a website that brings customers, managed for you, from $79/month.**

Launch in about two weeks with a clear mobile site, simple monthly pricing, and one person to call when something needs changing.

### Français

**Votre entreprise mérite mieux qu’une page Facebook — un site qui vous apporte des clients, géré pour vous, à partir de 79 $/mois.**

Mise en ligne en environ deux semaines, avec un site mobile clair, un prix mensuel simple et une personne à joindre quand quelque chose doit changer.

## Positioning rules

- Sell relief and ongoing responsibility, not hours of development.
- Present two simple subscriptions: Showcase at $79/month and Booking at $129/month.
- Make it clear that launch, domain, hosting, updates, and text/email support are included.
- The base site is written in one language of the client’s choice (English or French), included. Bilingual sites are discussed by custom quote.
- Keep Canada open as the service area while using Montreal-area roots as trust context.
- Explain the 12-month commitment, month-to-month continuation, 30-day notice, and early-transfer/deactivation choices.
- Do not imply quantified business results that have not been measured.
- Do not present custom applications, integrations, or agency-scale marketing as part of the managed website.

- French: “Textez-moi sur WhatsApp, je réponds aujourd’hui” / “Envoyer ma demande”

## Payment explanation

Showcase is $79/month. Booking is $129/month. Both include launch in about two weeks. The initial commitment is 12 months, then the subscription continues month to month with no new commitment and 30 days’ notice. If a client leaves early, they can pay a $500 transfer fee and receive ownership of the site, or have the site deactivated. The domain always remains theirs.
